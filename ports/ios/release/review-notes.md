## Notes for App Review

PHOBOS: Arena Survival is a single-player twin-stick survival shooter. No account or sign-in is needed, there are no ads and no in-app purchases, and the game plays fully offline — it does not use the network.

How to play (touch controls):
- In the main menu use the on-screen ▲ ▼ buttons and OK to choose GRAJ (Play).
- During a round, touch anywhere on the left half of the screen and drag to move.
- Touch and drag on the right half to aim; your exoskeleton fires automatically while you aim.
- The II button in the top-right corner pauses the game; the ♪ button in the pause screen mutes the sound.

Game controllers are optional: the game is fully playable with touch only. If a controller (e.g. DualSense, Xbox, MFi) is used, the touch buttons hide and come back on the next touch.

Licences: the main menu entry LICENCJE (Licences) shows the game licence (MIT), the full SIL Open Font License texts of the bundled fonts and the Godot Engine licences with all third-party notices. Game graphics were generated with AI (Claude) for this game.

The local TOP 10 leaderboard (WYNIKI) is stored only on the device. The user interface is in Polish; the store listing is available in Polish and English.
