## Notes for App Review

1. Screen recording (physical iPhone, latest iOS, starts with launching the app): https://youtu.be/WMCRJQ4CWC8
It shows the main menu, choosing the difficulty, a game round (moving, aiming and firing, collecting power-ups), pause and mute, game over with entering initials, the local SCORES table and the LICENSES screen. No account registration or login, no user-generated content, no paid content.

2. Purpose and audience: PHOBOS Survivor is a single-player twin-stick arena survival shooter. The player controls an exoskeleton and survives waves of fictional alien creatures, trying to beat their own best score. Entertainment for casual and arcade players aged 13+ (fantasy violence). It offers short, replayable sessions playable fully offline, with touch controls designed for iPhone and iPad.

3. How to use: No account or sign-in is needed, no sample files.
- Main menu: on-screen up/down arrow buttons and OK choose PLAY; left/right on DIFFICULTY change the difficulty.
- During a round, touch and drag on the left half of the screen to move; touch and drag on the right half to aim — the exoskeleton fires automatically while aiming.
- Green orbs restore health, blue orbs increase the fire rate.
- The pause button (two bars, top-right) pauses the game; on the pause screen the speaker button mutes the sound.
- After game over the player enters initials; SCORES shows the local TOP 10, stored only on the device.
- Game controllers (DualSense, Xbox, MFi) are optional: the game is fully playable with touch only. If a controller is used, the touch buttons hide and come back on the next touch.

4. External services: none. No servers, data providers, authentication, payments, analytics, ads or AI services. There are no in-app purchases; the game plays fully offline and does not use the network.

5. Regional differences: none, the app works the same in all regions. The game is available in Polish and English: the language follows the device setting (Polish on a Polish device, English otherwise).

Game graphics were generated with AI (Claude) for this game, which I may use commercially; sound and music are generated by the game.
