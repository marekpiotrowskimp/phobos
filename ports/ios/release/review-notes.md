## Notes for App Review

PHOBOS Survivor is a single-player twin-stick survival shooter. No account or sign-in is needed, there are no ads and no in-app purchases, and the game plays fully offline — it does not use the network.

How to play (touch controls):
- In the main menu use the on-screen up/down arrow buttons and OK to choose PLAY.
- During a round, touch anywhere on the left half of the screen and drag to move.
- Touch and drag on the right half to aim; your exoskeleton fires automatically while you aim.
- The pause button (two bars) in the top-right corner pauses the game; the speaker button next to it on the pause screen mutes the sound.

Game controllers are optional: the game is fully playable with touch only. If a controller (e.g. DualSense, Xbox, MFi) is used, the touch buttons hide and come back on the next touch.

Licenses: the main menu entry LICENSES (LICENCJE on a Polish device) shows the game licence (MIT), the full SIL Open Font License texts of the bundled fonts and the Godot Engine licences with all third-party notices. Game graphics were generated with AI (Claude) for this game.

The local TOP 10 leaderboard (SCORES) is stored only on the device. The game is available in Polish and English: the language follows the device setting (Polish on a Polish device, English otherwise).
