## Name
PHOBOS: Arena Survival

## Subtitle
Twin-stick survival shooter

## Promotional Text
One arena, alien waves from every side and a single goal: survive longer than last time. Beat your best score!

## Description
PHOBOS is a fast twin-stick shooter about surviving on a single enclosed arena. Alien organisms pour in from every side in waves, and each wave is faster and bigger than the last.

CONTROLS
• Left thumb moves your exoskeleton, right thumb aims and fires — comfortable touch controls designed from the ground up for iPhone and iPad.
• Gentle aim assist helps you hit targets during quick thumb movements.
• Prefer a gamepad? The game supports game controllers (such as DualSense and other iOS-compatible controllers). A controller is optional.

GAMEPLAY
• Waves of increasingly dangerous enemies with a visible, glowing core.
• Green capsules restore health, blue reactors briefly boost your fire rate.
• Three difficulty levels — from a relaxed run to a real challenge.
• Local TOP 10 leaderboard with initials and a difficulty filter.

NO STRINGS ATTACHED
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection.
• Plays fully offline.

Bio-industrial horror style: a dark metal arena overgrown by organic forms and procedurally generated music that builds up with the pressure on the arena.
