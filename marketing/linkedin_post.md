## Post

PHOBOS Survivor is now available on the App Store. 🎮

It's a fast twin-stick survival shooter for iPhone and iPad: one arena, alien waves from every side and a single goal — survive longer than last time.

What's inside:
🎯 Left thumb moves, right thumb aims and fires — controls designed for touch
🎯 Waves that get faster and bigger, with power-ups for health and fire rate
🎯 Three difficulty levels and a local TOP 10 leaderboard
🎯 Procedurally generated music that builds up with the action
🎯 Optional support for DualSense, Xbox and other controllers

Free, no ads, no in-app purchases, no account needed. Plays fully offline, in English and Polish.

Built with Godot, with graphics generated with AI (Claude). The code is open source on GitHub.
