PHOBOS Survivor Support

Welcome to PHOBOS Survivor support. Here you'll find answers to common questions and ways to get help.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Left thumb moves your exoskeleton, right thumb aims and fires. Survive the waves of the invasion — each wave cleared adds bonus points. Green orbs restore health, blue orbs speed up your fire rate.

How does movement work?

Movement follows the latest direction of your left thumb, so you can turn with a short stroke without looking at the stick. Keep your thumb still to keep running in the same direction; lift it to stop.

How do I pause the game?

Tap the II button in the top-right corner. The game also pauses automatically when you leave the app or receive a call.

How do I mute the sound?

Pause the game and tap the sound button.

Can I use a game controller?

Yes, a controller such as DualSense or an Xbox controller is optional. When you use it, the touch buttons hide; they come back when you touch the screen.

Where is my leaderboard saved?

The TOP 10 leaderboard is stored only on your device. Deleting the game deletes it.

Which languages are supported?

English and Polish. The game follows the language of your device.
