Privacy Policy for PHOBOS Survivor

Effective date: November 4, 2026

PHOBOS Survivor (“the game”) does not collect, store on servers or share any personal data or usage data.

Data Collection

  • The game requires no account, shows no ads and has no in-app purchases.
  • The game uses no analytics, no tracking and no third-party advertising libraries.
  • The game does not connect to the internet.

Data Storage

  • The leaderboard (TOP 10 with initials, score, wave and time) and settings such as the selected difficulty are stored only locally on the device. Deleting the game deletes this data.
  • Leaderboard initials are typed in by the player and are never sent anywhere.

Children's Privacy

  • The game does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

  • Changes to this policy will be published at this address with a new effective date.
